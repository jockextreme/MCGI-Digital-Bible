MCGI Digital Bible

Presenting the Holy Scriptures during Church services

Welcome. This is a simple tool for showing Bible verses on screen during Church services and related gatherings in the locale.

What this is

MCGI Digital Bible is a live verse presentation system.

An operator selects the verse. Brethren and guests may view it on a television, projector, or another device through the presenter link.

It is intended to keep the reading of the Holy Scriptures clear and easy to follow while the service is ongoing.

Why it was made

During Bible reading and the discussion of the biblical topic, verses are often cited one after another.

This was made to help the locale present those verses quickly and orderly on screen — through the help of God — so the congregation can follow the Holy Scriptures with less delay and less confusion.

Who it is for

For use in:

  • Locales and coordinating centers
  • Prayer Meeting
  • Worship Service
  • Thanksgiving
  • Bible Study / biblical discussion
  • Helpers assigned to the screen or second display

For:

  • Operators who control the verses
  • Viewers who only need to follow what is currently being shown

About the original

This follows the same general purpose as the original Digital Bible presentation page used for operator and viewer display: bible.mcgi.app

The aim remains the same: one side controls the verse, the other side displays it clearly for everyone to follow.

This version is arranged with a clearer operator console, a cleaner presenter view, and a layout that works on computers, tablets, and mobile phones.

How to use

If you are assigned as operator:

  1. Open the Control Panel
  2. Type in your Locale and Operator name — no account or password needed
  3. Select the verse to be shown
  4. Share the presenter link for the screen or helpers

If you are viewing only:

  1. Open the Presenter
  2. Follow the locale link
  3. The current verse will appear as it is selected
  4. No account is required for viewers
